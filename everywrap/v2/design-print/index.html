Not the cheapest print. On purpose.

The film
We print on 3M and Avery Dennison cast wrap film. It stretches into recesses and comes off cleanly years later, which cheaper film does not.
The laminate
A laminated print is sealed under a clear layer, so the ink is protected from fuel, washing and sun.
Printed by installers
We wrap with what we print. Panels are sized and overlapped to go on a vehicle, not just to come off a printer.

Design and print questions

How much does wrap printing cost per square foot?

3M wrap film is $8.43 per square foot printed and laminated, or $7.21 without laminate. Avery Dennison film is $8.85 laminated. Chrome prints are $16.86 and wall graphics $3.30.

How much does a custom wrap design cost?

A full custom design starts at $1,560. If you already have artwork, setting it up for print starts at $318.

How many square feet does it take to wrap a vehicle?

As a rough guide, a sedan takes 250 to 300 square feet of film, an SUV 300 to 375 and a cargo van 400 to 500, including the overlap and waste. We measure your exact vehicle for the quote.

What files do you need?

Vector files are best: AI, EPS or PDF with the fonts outlined. For photos, send the largest file you have and we tell you whether it will hold up at full size.

Do I need the print laminated?

For anything going on a vehicle or outdoors, yes. Print without laminate suits shops that laminate in-house, and short-term or indoor graphics.

Do you install what you print?

Yes. You can also order print only and install it yourself.

Can you design the wrap for me?

Yes. We design car wraps, van and fleet wraps and full brand identities, and you approve the design on your own vehicle before we print.

Can you print on chrome?

Yes. Chrome prints run on mirror-finish film and cost $16.86 per square foot.
