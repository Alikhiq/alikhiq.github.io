    Fleet wrap questions

    How much does a commercial vehicle wrap cost?

    Door logos and lettering start at $250. A partial wrap starts at $1,500, a full van wrap at $4,500, a box truck at $5,200 and a food truck or trailer at $5,200.

    Do you design the graphics?

    Yes. Send the logo and the vehicle list. Setting up artwork you already have starts at $318, and a full custom design at $1,560. The proof comes back with your price.

    Can you match one design across different vehicles?

    Yes. The design is laid out per vehicle so the brand reads the same on a van, a box truck and a trailer.

    How long does a fleet wrap last?

    Printed and laminated wraps are made to last years of daily use. Up to about seven is typical for a vehicle that is washed regularly.

    How long are the vehicles off the road?

    Installing a wrap on a van or box truck usually takes two to three days. For a fleet we stagger the vehicles so the work keeps moving.

    Can you wrap a leased van?

    Yes. A wrap comes off without marking sound factory paint, which is why leased fleets are wrapped and not painted.

    Do you do food trucks, trailers and boats?

    Yes. All three are in our work, including a full colour change and custom design on a food truck in Dublin.
