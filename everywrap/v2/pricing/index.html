What moves the price

  • Vehicle size. More panels and more glass mean more film and more hours.
  • The film. Colour-shift, chrome-look and textured wrap films cost more, and ceramic tint costs more than carbon.
  • Coverage. A roof is not a full wrap, and a partial front is not full body protection film.
  • Condition. Chipped paint, old tint and poor resprays need dealing with before new film goes on.

Starting prices for vehicles with sound paint and glass. Size, film and condition move the number, so your exact price comes in a written quote before any work starts.

Pricing questions

Are these the final prices?

They are starting prices for vehicles in good condition. Your exact price comes in a written quote, and nothing starts until you agree to it.

How much does it cost to wrap a car in Columbus?

A full colour change starts at $2,499 for a coupe or sedan, $3,400 for an SUV or truck and $4,000 for a van or large SUV.

Why do colour-shift and chrome-look wraps cost more?

The film itself costs more per roll and takes more care to install, so specialty films start at $3,600.

Is it cheaper to do a wrap, PPF and tint together?

It saves you time, because the car is off the road once. Ask for all three in one quote and we price them as one job.

How do I get an exact price?

Tap Book now, tell us the car and what you want done, and you get the price in writing by text or call.
