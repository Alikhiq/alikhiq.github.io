    Vinyl wrap, plainly.

    A wrap changes the colour of your car without repainting it. We cover it panel by panel in vinyl film and tuck the edges, so the old colour does not show at the doors and seams.

    Finishes
    Satin, gloss, matte, colour-shift and printed designs. Satin black is the most requested.
    Coverage
    Full colour change, or parts: roof, hood, stripes, chrome delete.
    Film

    Your paint
    The film sits on the factory paint. We look at the paint first, because film will not hide damage and can lift a poor respray.

    Questions people ask

    Will a wrap damage my paint?

    On sound factory paint, quality film comes off cleanly. Repainted or chipped panels are the risk, so we inspect the car before we quote and tell you what we see.

    Can you wrap just part of the car?

    Yes. Roofs, hoods, stripes and chrome delete are all common jobs in our archive.

    How is a wrap priced?

    By the size of the vehicle, the film you choose and how much of the car is covered. You get the price in writing before any work starts.

    How do I look after it?

    Hand wash, no abrasive polish, and keep fuel and bird droppings from sitting on the film. We give you care instructions with the job.
