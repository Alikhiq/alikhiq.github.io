    A new colour, and your paint stays underneath.

    A vinyl wrap changes the colour of your car without repainting it. We lay the film one panel at a time and tuck the edges, so the old colour does not show at the doors and seams. When you want the original colour back, the film comes off.

    Finishes
    Satin, gloss, matte and colour-shift. Satin black is the most requested, and we have wrapped cars in pink, Tiffany blue, purple and chrome-look green too.
    Full or partial
    A full colour change, or one part of the car: roof, hood, mirrors, stripes or a chrome delete.
    Film
    We install cast film from 3M, Avery Dennison and KPMF. Tell us if you have a colour code in mind.
    Your paint first
    Film follows whatever is under it. We check the paint before we quote, because film will not hide chips and can lift a poor respray.

    How a wrap sits on the car

    The film bonds to the paint, not into it. That is why it can come off.

    What moves the price

    • Vehicle size, and how deep the bumpers, mirrors and vents are
    • The film: colour-shift, chrome-look and textured films cost more per roll
    • Extras such as door jambs, which add time
    • Paint condition: chips, peeling clear coat and resprays need dealing with first

    Starting prices for vehicles with sound paint and glass. Size, film and condition move the number, so your exact price comes in a written quote before any work starts.

    Good to know

    How long it takes
    Most full wraps are in the shop for a few days. A roof, hood or chrome delete is a much shorter visit. You get the dates with your price.
    How long it lasts
    Film makers rate quality cast vinyl for about five to seven years outdoors. Garage parking and regular hand washing get you the long end of that.
    Looking after it
    Hand wash or use a touchless wash. Skip brush washes, abrasive polish and wax on satin or matte film, and wipe off fuel and bird droppings quickly.

    Car wrap questions

    How much does it cost to wrap a car in Columbus, Ohio?

    A full colour change starts at $2,499 for a coupe or sedan, $3,400 for an SUV or truck and $4,000 for a van or large SUV. Colour-shift and specialty films start at $3,600. You get your exact price in writing before any work starts.

    How long does a car wrap last?

    Film makers rate quality cast vinyl for about five to seven years outdoors. Sun, road salt and how the car is washed decide where in that range yours lands.

    Will a wrap damage my paint?

    On sound factory paint, quality film comes off cleanly and the paint underneath has been shielded the whole time. Repainted or chipped panels are the risk, so we check the car before we quote and tell you what we see.

    Is wrapping a car cheaper than painting it?

    For a colour change of the same quality, usually yes. A wrap also comes off, so the factory colour is still there when you sell the car.

    Can you wrap just part of the car?

    Yes. A roof starts at $350, a hood at $350 and a chrome delete at $350.

    Can a wrapped car go through a car wash?

    Touchless washes are fine. Brush washes can scratch the film and catch an edge, so we tell people to avoid them.

    Can you wrap a leased car?

    Yes, and it is a common reason to wrap, because the film comes off before the car goes back. Read your lease first.

    How long does it take to wrap a car?

    A few days for most full wraps. You get the dates with your price.

    What finishes are available?

    Gloss, satin, matte, colour-shift, chrome-look and textured films such as carbon-look. Satin black is the one people ask for most.
