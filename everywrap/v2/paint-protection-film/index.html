    Clear film that takes the hit, so the paint does not.

    Paint protection film, also called PPF or a clear bra, is a clear urethane layer over the paint. It takes the stone chips, bug marks and light scratches of Ohio roads, and it is far cheaper to replace than a respray.

    Partial front
    The bumper, plus the leading edge of the hood and fenders. The lowest-cost way to cover where chips land first.
    Full front
    The bumper, the whole hood, both fenders and the mirrors, with no line across the hood. The most popular choice.
    Full body
    Every painted panel. For a car you plan to keep, or one you want to hand back unmarked.
    With a wrap or tint
    Protection film, a colour wrap and tint can be planned as one job, so the car is off the road once.

    Good to know

    How long it takes
    A full front is usually two to three days. Full body takes most of a week.
    How long it lasts
    Modern film is made to stay clear for years, and the makers back it with long warranties. We tell you the warranty for the film you pick.
    Looking after it
    Wait about a week before the first wash, then wash it like paint. Light swirl marks in self-healing film soften with warmth.

    PPF questions

    How much does paint protection film cost in Columbus, Ohio?

    A partial front starts at $1,100, a full front at $1,900 and full body at $5,500 for a coupe or sedan. SUVs and trucks cost a little more because there is more to cover.

    Is PPF worth it?

    If you drive on the highway, yes for the front of the car at least. That is where stone chips land, and film costs less than repainting a bumper and hood.

    Can you see the film?

    On clean paint it is hard to spot. Edges are placed along panel lines where they are least visible, and a full front has no line across the hood.

    Which coverage should I choose?

    Full front covers the area that takes most of the damage. Full body is for a car you plan to keep a long time. Tell us how you use the car and we will say what we would do.

    What is the difference between PPF and a ceramic coating?

    Film is a physical layer that stops chips and scratches. A coating is a thin chemical layer that makes the car easier to clean but will not stop a stone. Many people put a coating over film.

    Does PPF damage paint when it comes off?

    Not on sound factory paint. As with a wrap, repainted panels are where the risk is, and we check for that first.

    Does PPF yellow over time?

    Old films did. Modern urethane film is made to stay clear, and the makers back that with warranties of ten years or more on their main lines.

    Can I take my car through a car wash with PPF?

    Yes, after the film has cured. Touchless or hand washing is kindest to it.

    Can you put a ceramic coating over PPF?

    Yes. A coating over film makes it easier to clean and is a common pairing.

    Will PPF show on a black car?

    Black shows everything, so edge placement matters most there. Edges go along panel lines, and a full front avoids a line across the hood.
