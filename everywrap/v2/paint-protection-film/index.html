    Paint protection film, plainly.

    Paint protection film is a clear layer over the paint. It takes the stone chips, bug marks and light scratches so the paint underneath does not.

    Partial front
    The bumper and the leading edge of the hood and fenders.
    Full front
    The bumper, the whole hood, both fenders and the mirrors.
    Full body
    Every painted panel.
    With a wrap or tint
    Protection film, a colour wrap and tint can be planned as one job, so the car is only off the road once.

    Questions people ask

    Can you see the film?

    On clean paint it is hard to spot. Edges are placed along panel lines where they are least visible.

    Which coverage should I choose?

    Full front covers the area that takes most of the damage on the road. Full body is for a car you plan to keep a long time. Tell us how you use the car and we will say what we would do.

    How is it priced?

    By the panels covered and the size of the vehicle. You get the price in writing first.
