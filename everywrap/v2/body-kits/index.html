    Body kits, plainly.

    A kit changes the shape of the car: splitter, skirts, spoiler, wider arches. Tell us what you drive and we look for a kit made for that car, then fit it and wrap it so it matches.

    Find
    We look for a kit made for your exact model and year.
    Fit
    Test fit first, then install.
    Finish
    Wrapped with the rest of the car, or in a contrast finish such as gloss black or carbon.

    What it costs

    Every vehicle is priced on its own, so there is no flat number here. You get a written quote first, and nothing starts until you agree to it.

    Questions people ask

    Do you supply the kit?

    We can source it, or fit one you already have. Send the year, make and model and we will tell you what is available.

    Can it be removed later?

    That depends on the kit. Bolt-on parts come off; parts that are bonded or need cutting are permanent. We tell you which before you commit.

    How is it priced?

    The kit, the fitting and the wrap are quoted separately so you can see each one. All in writing first.
