    Ask by voice

    Tell us the car. We find the kit, fit it and finish it.

    A kit changes the shape of the car: splitter, skirts, diffuser, spoiler, wider arches. Send the year, make and model and we look for a kit made for that car, test fit it, install it and wrap it so it matches.

    Find
    We look for a kit made for your exact model and year, or fit one you already have.
    Fit
    Test fit first, then install. Bolt-on where possible, and we tell you before anything needs cutting or bonding.
    Finish
    Wrapped with the rest of the car, or in a contrast finish such as gloss black or carbon-look.

    What moves the price

    • The kit itself, and how well it is made
    • Bolt-on or bonded, and whether panels need trimming
    • Whether we source the kit or you bring it
    • The finish: wrapped to match, contrast, or left in carbon

    Starting prices for vehicles with sound paint and glass. Size, film and condition move the number, so your exact price comes in a written quote before any work starts.

    Good to know

    How long it takes
    A lip or splitter is usually a same-day job. A full kit depends on the fit and is planned with the wrap.
    Reversible or not
    Bolt-on parts come off. Parts that are bonded or need cutting are permanent, and we say which before you commit.
    Ground clearance
    Lips and splitters sit low. We talk through driveways and speed bumps before you choose.

    Body kit questions

    How much does it cost to install a body kit?

    It depends on the kit and how it attaches, so we quote per car. The kit, the fitting and the wrap are priced separately so you can see each one.

    Do you supply the kit?

    We can source it, or fit one you already have. Send the year, make and model and we will tell you what is available.

    Can it be removed later?

    Bolt-on parts come off. Parts that are bonded or need cutting are permanent. We tell you which before you commit.

    Can you wrap the kit to match my car?

    Yes. The kit can be wrapped with the rest of the car, or in a contrast finish such as gloss black.

    Will a kit fit my car?

    Kits are made for a specific model and year. Send yours and we check fitment before anything is ordered.
