    Questions people ask

    How much does it cost to wrap a car in Columbus, Ohio?

    A full colour change starts at $2,499 for a coupe or sedan, $3,400 for an SUV or truck and $4,000 for a van or large SUV. Colour-shift and specialty films start at $3,600. Roofs, hoods and chrome delete are priced on their own.

    How long does a car wrap last?

    Film makers rate quality cast vinyl for about five to seven years outdoors. Garage parking and hand washing get you the long end of that.

    Is a wrap cheaper than a paint job?

    For a colour change of the same quality, usually yes. A wrap also comes off, so the factory paint is still there underneath.

    What window tint is legal in Ohio?

    Front side windows must let at least 50% of light through. Rear side windows and the back glass can be any darkness. On the windshield, only a strip across the top five inches can be dark.

    How much is paint protection film?

    A partial front starts at $1,100, a full front at $1,900 and full body at $5,500 for a coupe or sedan.

    Can I see it on my own car first?

    Yes. Upload a photo and pick a finish, and we send your car back in that finish with your price.

    Do you wrap business vehicles?

    Yes. Vans, box trucks, buses, food trucks and boats, from door lettering at $250 to full printed wraps. See the commercial page.
