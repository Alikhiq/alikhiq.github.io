    Window tint, plainly.

    Tint cuts glare and heat and gives the car a finished look. The number is how much light gets through: 70% is light, 5% is limo dark.

    70%
    Light. Barely changes the look, takes the edge off the sun.
    35%
    Medium. A clear step darker than factory glass.
    20%
    Dark. The common choice for rear glass.
    5%
    Limo. Very dark, for rear windows.

    What it costs

    Every vehicle is priced on its own, so there is no flat number here. You get a written quote first, and nothing starts until you agree to it.

    Questions people ask

    What is legal in Ohio?

    Front side windows must let in at least 50% of light. Rear side windows and the back glass can go darker. We confirm what applies to your car before we cut film.

    Can I roll the windows down afterwards?

    Not straight away. The film needs time to cure, and we tell you how long when you collect the car.

    How is tint priced?

    By the number of windows and the film. You get the price in writing first.
