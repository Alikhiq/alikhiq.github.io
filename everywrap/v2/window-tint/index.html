    Window tint questions

    How much does window tint cost in Columbus, Ohio?

    A full car in carbon film starts at $280 and in ceramic at $450. Two front windows to match factory rear glass start at $120.

    What is the legal tint in Ohio?

    Front side windows must let at least 50% of light through. Rear side windows and the back glass can be any darkness. On the windshield, only a strip across the top five inches can be dark. Reflective film is not allowed on any window. We confirm what applies to your car before we cut film.

    Is 20% tint legal in Ohio?

    On the rear side windows and the back glass, yes. On the front side windows, no: those need to let in at least 50%.

    Is ceramic tint worth the extra?

    If heat is the reason you want tint, yes. Ceramic blocks noticeably more heat than carbon at the same shade, and it does not interfere with phone or radio signal.

    How long does it take to tint a car?

    Most cars are done the same day.

    When can I roll my windows down?

    Give it a few days so the film can cure. We tell you exactly how long when you collect the car.

    Can you remove old tint?

    Yes. Old, purple or bubbling tint is removed before the new film goes on. Removal starts at $100.
