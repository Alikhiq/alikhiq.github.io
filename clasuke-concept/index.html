Tidepool
Concept for Claytom · November 2026

Every sound gets a color and a shape.

A music studio for his PC where you watch the song instead of reading menus. Press play: drums, bass, keys and voice each flow as their own river. Tap a river to mute it, tap the ring to change the beat, or drop in one of his own songs. Hit ES in the window to see it in Spanish.

What he can do in it

Six tools, each one a picture first

Each mode is built on an open-source project that runs on his own PC. No uploads, no credits, no waiting in a queue.

Make

Pick mood colors and type a few words. Get back a full song or a loop with stems. Don't like bars 9 to 16? Paint over them and regenerate just that part.

ACE-Step 1.5HeartMuLa (alt)runs locally

Split

Drop any song and it comes apart into drums, bass, vocals and the rest, each as its own river he can mute, solo or drag out.

DemucsRoFormer models via audio-separator

Voice

Train a model on his own voice once. Then hum or rough out a part and hear it come back in his voice. The voice shows as a color, so a swap is easy to see.

Applio (RVC)his own voice only

Notes

Hum a melody or pick a stem, and it turns into MIDI blocks he can drag straight into his beat software.

Basic Pitch (Spotify)key + BPM: Essentia

Finish

Pick a song he loves as the target. His track gets matched to its loudness and tone, and both show side by side so he can see how close they are.

Matcheringreference mastering

Live

He already streams on Kick. One switch sends the rivers and bloom to OBS as an overlay, so chat watches the beat take shape. His own tracks become stream music and intro stingers, instead of other people's songs.

How we'd build it

One installer he double-clicks

He gets a Windows installer. The look above is web code, so this page's visuals carry straight into the real app.

Shell

Tauri 2 desktop app with a small .exe or .msi installer, in Spanish and English

The screen he sees. Rivers, ring, wheel and bloom are drawn with canvas, like this page. The same view serves a local page that OBS can add as a browser source.

Engines

A Python helper bundled with the app that runs the models

ACE-Step, Demucs, Applio, Basic Pitch and Matchering. Models download once on first launch, then everything works offline.

His files

One project folder per song

Stems, MIDI and masters export as plain WAV and MIDI files that open in FL Studio, Ableton or whatever he uses.

His PC

Needs a graphics card for generation and voice

Splitting, notes and mastering run on most PCs. Song generation and voice training are the heavy parts, so we check his specs before promising speed.

Majik's Music StudioApache-2.0 · good starting template

Tauri 2 + Svelte app already wired to ACE-Step 1.5, with a library and stem separation. Closest thing to a ready base.

StemDeckApache-2.0 · reuse for Split

Desktop stem splitter on Demucs, up to six stems, fully local.

Pymss StudioAGPL-3.0 · reference only

Polished separation workspace in Tauri 2. Copying its code would force us to open-source his app, so we'd only borrow ideas.

Before we build

Six things to ask Claytom

His answers decide which modes come first and what the home screen shows.

Where does he make beats now?FL Studio, Ableton, BandLab, a phone app? Exports should drop straight into it.
Which AI tools does he use, and for what?Suno, Udio, stem sites, voice tools. We replace those first.
What part does he hate doing?That becomes the first button on the home screen.
Does he sing, and would he make music live on Kick?Decides how much goes to the Voice and Live modes.
What's in his PC?Windows version and graphics card model. This sets which models we ship.
Two or three of his songsTo load into this demo and tune the colors and visuals around his sound.

What his public pages show: his Kick channel streams casual Just Chatting in Spanish, with no saved videos or music streams yet.
